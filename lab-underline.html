A line, drawn.

Three ways the same pen stroke can arrive. Hover each one, and tab through them too — the keyboard path is the same interaction.

1 · Draw only

The stroke is laid out at full width and revealed left to right in 210ms, so it arrives already uneven rather than growing into shape. Try this link, or read it in a sentence: the work at Booking.com is where the habit started.

Nothing moves once drawn. The quietest of the three.

2 · Draw, then boil

Same entrance, then the line is redrawn — five separately generated strokes cut between at 8.3fps, the way hand-inked animation boils when every cel is traced afresh. Hold the cursor here: the mark never moves, its bends change. In a sentence: I made Latinxs Who Design because the conversations weren’t being recorded.

Nothing translates, rotates or scales. Endpoints are anchored across all five frames, so only the interior changes — a redrawn line, not a different line.

3 · Draw, then settle once

The line overshoots very slightly and comes to rest, the way a hand lifts off — hover here. In a sentence: the details matter because that is where the earlier decisions become real.

Motion happens once, then stops. Reads as deliberate rather than animated.

4 · Marker, not pen

Same draw, same settle — a heavier mark. The stroke swells and thins along its run and lifts at both ends, so it reads as a marker rather than a ruled line: hover here. In a sentence: I spent three years at Shopify before Atlassian.

Generated as a filled outline rather than a constant-width stroke — a plain stroke cannot change thickness along its length.

5 · Marker, boiling (baseline)

The heavier mark, boiling — hold here. In a sentence: the thing I keep coming back to is the whole product.

Thickness varies frame to frame as well as bend, so the nib appears to have been re-pressed each time.

5A · Three shapes, smooth morph

Three hand-drawn states, interpolated continuously rather than cut between — hold the cursor here. In a sentence: the thing I keep coming back to is the whole product.

1050ms per loop, linear throughout. The contour, the pressure and both ends are all moving at every instant; nothing ever holds still.

5B · Eight shapes, smooth morph

The same treatment across eight states, so the path wanders further before it returns — hold here. In a sentence: three years at Shopify before Atlassian.

1600ms per loop. Against 5A: three states return to the same shape often enough to sense a cycle, eight does not.
