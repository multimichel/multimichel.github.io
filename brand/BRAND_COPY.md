## LinkedIn About
I've spent my career making digital products and thinking about everything around them.

Since 1998, that's taken me from early web design in Brazil to product teams at Booking.com, Shopify, Atlassian and Xero, and now to Figma, where I work as a Designer Advocate.

I care about the whole product: whether we're asking the right question, what we're learning from people, how teams make decisions, how the system fits together, and whether the actual experience holds up. That means I find myself moving between design craft, experimentation, tools, systems, leadership and the stories we tell about our work.

I write, speak, teach and mentor because ideas become more useful when we share them, challenge them and learn from other people. I also created Latinxs Who Design, a podcast for conversations and perspectives that deserve more space in our industry.

These days I'm particularly curious about what AI changes about making software, and what it doesn't. Making something faster is genuinely exciting. Knowing whether it's the right thing to make is still the interesting part.

More of my work, writing and talks: https://multimichel.com/

## Figma Experience
Helping designers and teams explore new ways of making digital products, especially where design systems, code and AI-assisted workflows meet.

I work across talks, workshops, demos, writing and community programmes in Australia, New Zealand and the wider Asia-Pacific region. I spend time listening to how people actually work, turning unfamiliar technology into practical examples, and bringing those conversations back to the people building the tools.

I'm interested in what tools make possible, but even more in the decisions that make what we create useful, understandable and worth making.
