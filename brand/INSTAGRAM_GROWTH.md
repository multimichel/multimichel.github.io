# @multimichel — Instagram growth playbook
_Last updated 1 November 2026. Owner: Michel Ferreira._

## The job Instagram should do
**LinkedIn = professional credibility and opportunity.**
**MultiMichel.com = canonical archive and proof.**
**Instagram = personality, discovery, connection and creative process.**
It is not a second corporate LinkedIn feed. It should make people curious about Michel's work and who he is.

**Audience:** designers, makers, design leaders, design-curious developers, speakers, international design community (English primary; occasional Portuguese in a natural voice). Do not try to reach everybody.

**First milestone:** cross 1,000 genuine followers, then 1,500, then 3,000. The current count is **not verified** by public scraping; Michel reports fewer than 1,000 as of Nov 1, 2026. Treat milestones as targets, not promises or comparisons to coworkers.

## The recurring series
1. **Made me think** — one real design observation from the world: airport, shop, transit, children's toy, hotel, app. A visual and a question. Not every post needs to be about Figma.
2. **Behind the slide** — 20–45 sec video about a single idea from a talk: what changed after rehearsal, what the audience asked, or why a metaphor survived.
3. **Making in public** — short clips of prototypes, imperfect experiments, design systems, AI workflows, side projects; show what surprised you, not generic 'AI will change everything.'
4. **MultiMichel moments** — travels, people, visual inspirations, Brazil/Australia and speaking. Keep family details private and get permission for identifiable colleagues/event attendees.
5. **Small ideas, big arguments** — carousels that can stand alone without a linked essay. Examples: Fast ≠ Good, judgment beats production, tools should expand imagination, a shared definition of 'done.'

## Sustainable starting cadence (4-week experiment)
- **1 reel / week** (15–45 sec, one thought, subtitles, strong first 2 seconds).
- **1 carousel / week** (5–7 slides, practical/shareable).
- **1 candid photo / short personal post / week**, including talks/travel or a real observation.
- **Stories 2–4 days per week**, short spontaneous posts, polls, audience questions, event moments. They deepen relationships; don't rely on them alone for discovery.
- **15 minutes, 3× a week** responding and leaving thoughtful comments in your existing communities.
- **1 collaboration every 2–4 weeks** with another speaker, organiser or designer where it is authentic and appropriate, using Instagram's Collab feature.
- Batch-select from existing assets once per week; don't invent a whole photoshoot.

Studies find Instagram has separate feed and recommendation systems; watch time, likes per reach, and sends per reach matter. Reels are a discovery opportunity; carousels hold people for useful ideas. Avoid manufactured engagement bait and follower purchases.

## Four-week measurement sheet (use actual Instagram Insights / Metricool)
Log once every Sunday:
- follower count and **net new followers**
- accounts reached, **non-follower reach** and profile visits
- reel **average watch time** (not merely view count)
- **sends/shares per reach** (especially for carousels/reels)
- follows generated per piece, if accessible
- 3 best-performing *topics* (not just formats)
- time invested per week

Don't chase daily noise. After 4 weeks, repeat the best 2 topics and discontinue what feels performative or ineffective. If a professional account is available, use the Instagram dashboard's Best Practices and Insights. Where eligible, Trial Reels can test a video with non-followers before making it public.

## Opportunities to share existing audiences
- Link Instagram from the website: **already present in MultiMichel footer** (confirmed from source on Nov 1).
- Add canonical Instagram to website `Person.sameAs`.
- Link Instagram from relevant LinkedIn posts **when the Instagram version is genuinely different** (behind-the-scenes video, travel detail, extra photos), not on every LinkedIn post.
- Ask event organisers to tag/collaborate when your session or photos genuinely appear.
- Include handle on speaker kit and at the end of public talk recordings if appropriate.
- Repurpose **one idea, multiple manifestations**: talk → 30sec reel → 5-slide carousel → full essay/site archive.

## Boundaries
- Never buy followers or run follow/unfollow spam.
- Don't post private team discussions, unpublished work, identifiable attendee images without consent, or family logistics.
- Don't pretend to be 'on' every day. Interesting, original and personal beats forced consistency.
- Some stories can stay in your camera roll.
- Reassess growth based on audience quality, not a colleague's follower count.

## Open dependencies
- Public web access did not expose the Instagram post grid or exact follower total. Account-level audit and a real growth baseline require Instagram Insights or a linked creator analytics tool such as Metricool.
- Instagram bio, pins and external profile changes are **not** changed by a GitHub commit.
- Publishing creative assets requires selection, actual rights to the clips/images, and an authenticated publishing channel.
